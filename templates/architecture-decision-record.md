# Architecture Decision Record Template

## Title

Short descriptive title of the decision.

## Context

Describe the client environment, systems, and risks.

## Decision

State the chosen architecture or control and the reason for it.

## Considered options

- Option 1: description and why it was rejected.
- Option 2: description and why it was rejected.
- Option 3: description and why it was chosen.

## Tradeoffs

Explain the key tradeoffs, including security, cost, complexity, and operations.

## Outcome

What changed, what improvements were delivered, and how success was measured.

## Notes

- Keep all data anonymized.
- Replace sensitive values with placeholders.
- Focus on reasoning, not raw configuration.
