# Outcome

## What changed

- The Prisma-managed secret was rotated and replaced in connector configurations.
- CyberArk vault entries were updated with the new Prisma credential.
- Old Prisma credentials were retired only after validation completed.

## Results

- Prisma policy enforcement workflows stabilized.
- Automated security checks resumed without interruption.
- The security team gained a documented Prisma secret rotation process.
- The organization reduced the risk of stale credentials in the secret lifecycle.
