# Outcome

## What changed

- Okta became the unified identity provider for AWS and Azure access.
- AWS SSO and Azure AD federation were configured with standardized role-to-group mappings.
- Conditional access policies were enforced centrally in Okta for MFA and session controls.
- Onboarding and offboarding were automated through Okta provisioning.

## Results

- Users now have a consistent sign-on experience across both clouds.
- Cloud IAM configurations are simpler and better aligned with governance.
- Audit and access reviews improved because entitlement mappings are centralized.
- The organization reduced the risk of orphaned accounts and unmanaged privileged access.
